A useful first distinction

Surgical or non-surgical?

The right choice depends on the tissue involved, the degree of change you want, your health and the recovery you can accommodate. A consultation should make that trade-off clear.

Surgical procedures

When structural change is needed

Surgery may be considered when the concern involves bone, cartilage, gland, muscle, significant skin laxity or fat that needs direct removal.

  • Usually creates a larger or more durable change
  • Requires medical screening and a defined recovery period
  • Includes scars and procedure-specific risks
Non-surgical treatments

When a smaller change may be enough

Injectables, threads, lasers and energy-based treatments can suit selected concerns where surgery is not needed or not desired.

  • Often involves less downtime
  • Results may be gradual, temporary or session-based
  • Still requires qualified assessment and informed consent

How much does plastic surgery cost in Delhi?

There is no responsible single price for every patient. Cost depends on the procedure, complexity, facility, anaesthesia, implants or consumables and follow-up needs. Request a written, itemised estimate after examination. Do not choose surgery on price alone.

How do I choose a qualified plastic surgeon in Delhi?
Check for a recognised specialist qualification such as MCh or DNB in Plastic Surgery, verify current medical registration, ask who will perform the procedure and confirm where it will take place. Review procedure-specific experience and discuss risks, alternatives and follow-up before deciding.
What happens during a plastic surgery consultation?
The surgeon reviews your concern, medical history, medicines, previous treatments and expectations, then examines the relevant area. If treatment may be suitable, you should receive an explanation of options, limitations, risks, recovery and likely cost. A consultation does not oblige you to proceed.
Can I have an initial video consultation?
A video conversation can help patients travelling from outside Delhi discuss goals and logistics. A final recommendation and surgical plan normally require an in-person examination and any relevant medical tests.
Will my result look exactly like a before and after photograph?
No. Photographs show what happened for another patient. Your anatomy, skin, health, procedure and healing are different. Use images to discuss direction and technique, not as a promise of a specific outcome.
How long does recovery take?
Recovery varies widely by procedure and patient. Some non-surgical treatments involve little interruption, while operations can require days or weeks away from work and longer for swelling to settle. Your surgeon should give a procedure-specific timeline and warning signs.
Does health insurance cover cosmetic surgery?
Procedures performed only to change appearance are commonly self-funded. Reconstructive or function-related treatment may be considered differently by an insurer. Confirm coverage directly with your insurer before treatment.
